Support

Need help with Butler? We're a small team and read every message ourselves.

Contact us

engineering@butler-app.com

We typically reply within a couple of business days. Include your account email and as much detail as you can (what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead) so we can help faster.

How do I report a bug or send feedback?

In the app, shake your phone from any screen, or go to the Me tab and tap "Send feedback." You can attach a screenshot directly. You can also email us at engineering@butler-app.com.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, and select Butler to change your plan or cancel.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email us at engineering@butler-app.com from your account address and we'll take care of it.
