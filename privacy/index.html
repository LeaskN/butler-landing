Privacy Policy

Last updated August 11, 2026

Butler is a voice-first personal AI assistant. This page explains what data Butler collects, why, who we share it with to make the app work, and how you can control or remove it. We only collect what's needed to run the features you actually use.

Data you provide directly

  • Account info — email and password, or your name/email if you sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Profile info — your name and, optionally, a preferred nickname.
  • Conversations with Butler — chat messages and voice transcripts, so Butler can respond and recall context in later conversations.
  • Goals, reminders, and memories — anything you ask Butler to track or remember.
  • Wardrobe and food photos — used to suggest outfits and log meals; processed to extract the information you asked for.
  • Feedback reports — bug reports or ideas you send from the app, optionally with a screenshot.

Data collected with your permission

  • Microphone — only while you're actively talking to Butler.
  • Location — used for weather and local context in Butler's responses.
  • Calendar — if you connect Google or Microsoft Calendar, Butler reads events to prepare briefings and suggest appointments.
  • Camera and photo library — for your profile picture, wardrobe photos, and meal logging.
  • Notifications — to deliver reminders and check-ins you've set up.

You control all of these in your device Settings at any time. Butler does not currently read or write Apple Health data, even though the app requests the permission for planned future features — no health data is collected today.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Before Butler first sends your information to the AI and voice providers above (Anthropic, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, and Tavily), we ask for your consent in the app and tell you what is shared and with whom. You can read this at any time in the app under Settings. We only work with providers that are contractually committed to protect your data to a standard equivalent to this policy, to use it solely to provide their service to Butler, and not to use it to train their own models.

How long we keep your data

We retain your account data and conversation history for as long as your account is active, so Butler can keep working the way you expect. If you delete your account or ask us to remove your data, we'll delete it within a reasonable time after verifying the request.

Your rights

You can permanently delete your account and everything tied to it at any time, right from the app, under Settings → Privacy → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and removes your account, your conversations, your uploaded photos, and all other data tied to your account. You can also request a copy of your data, or email eng@butler-app.com from your account's email address and we'll take care of it.

Children's privacy

Butler is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including database-level access controls that restrict every user to their own data, to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data appropriately.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email eng@butler-app.com.
